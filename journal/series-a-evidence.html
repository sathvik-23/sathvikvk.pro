What evidence earns a Series A?

Series A evidence is not simply more activity; it is a credible path from early demand to repeatable economics.

Investment question

As inference prices fall and model capability spreads, which constraints retain pricing power, technical defensibility and venture-scale ownership?

Conclusion

I would want evidence of customer retention, a repeatable sales or deployment motion, improving unit economics and a milestone the next round can underwrite.

VALUE MIGRATION
Models / tokens↓ cost, rising abundance
Next bottleneckpower · silicon · deployment · workflow
Investment testpricing power × ownership × fund-return relevance

Evidence

  • Inference cost declined by roughly 280× over the period tracked in the portfolio [S26].
  • India represented 1.7% of global industrial-robot installs in 2024 [S22].
  • The DLI scheme had sanctioned 24 projects and 16 tape-outs [S33].

Counterargument

Incumbent platforms may reach down and commoditise many apparent bottlenecks. A physical interface is not automatically a moat; the company must own deployment data, customer integration or a hard-to-replace component.

What would prove this wrong?

If compute-layer companies consistently capture the margin while physical-interface companies remain sub-scale component vendors, the thesis is wrong. I would update on gross-margin trends, design-in conversion and strategic acquisition evidence.

Investment implication

Track the bottleneck, then underwrite the ownership and price. A good company is not necessarily a good venture investment.
