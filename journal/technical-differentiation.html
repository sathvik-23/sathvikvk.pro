When does technical differentiation become customer value?

A technical edge matters only when a buyer pays for the resulting outcome.

Investment question

If demand for AI compute scales faster than power can be delivered to the rack, which layer captures the economic value?

Conclusion

Differentiation becomes customer value when it changes a customer metric, survives qualification and creates willingness to pay that alternatives cannot match.

POWER CONVERSION GATE
Technical claim→ working silicon
Commercial proof→ OEM design-in
Scale proof→ production volume and margin

Case in the universe

C2i Semiconductors is a WATCH, not a PASS or PROCEED. The trigger is working silicon plus credible OEM design-in. That is the difference between a power bottleneck and an investable power bottleneck.

Counterargument

System integrators and incumbent semiconductor suppliers may absorb the value. Power delivery can also become a feature of a larger platform rather than a standalone venture outcome.

What would change my mind?

Verified first-silicon performance, repeatable customer demand and evidence of pricing power after qualification would move the company forward.

Investment implication

Underwrite conversion, not category excitement. The kill question is simple: does a design win become production revenue?
