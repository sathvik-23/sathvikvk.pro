2027 RESEARCH AGENDA · THREE PRIORITIES

Where I am looking next.

These are research priorities, not guaranteed investment winners. I use them to decide which founders deserve a closer conversation.

01 / MEMORY & CONTEXT INFRASTRUCTURE

Can a system remember what a workflow actually needs?

Customer and why now

AI products need durable context across people, projects and time. Buyers include product teams and enterprises whose work cannot be re-explained every session.

Where value could accrue

Accuracy under temporal change, permissions, portability and evaluation—not merely storing more text.

Counterargument

Retrieval, structured application state and model-provider features may solve enough of the problem.

Pre-seed / seed evidence

A narrow workflow, measurable memory accuracy, permission-safe context and repeated use that beats a simpler baseline.

Invalidation: simpler retrieval or native platform memory consistently delivers the same outcome at lower implementation cost.

Starting sources: Mem0 memory evaluation, Sequoia: Own Your Intelligence.

02 / RELIABLE AI DEPLOYMENT

Can the system complete the task reliably, safely and economically?

Customer and why now

Teams moving from demos to production need evaluations, monitoring, security, orchestration and clear ownership when tasks fail.

Where value could accrue

Performance against real task outcomes, not model scores; cost per successful completion; and operational trust.

Counterargument

Simple workflows, internal tooling and model-provider platforms may absorb much of the stack.

Pre-seed / seed evidence

Multi-turn evals tied to customer outcomes, production adoption, controlled failure modes and a credible security posture.

Invalidation: customers cannot show improved task completion or the product becomes a thin dashboard around existing platform features.

Starting sources: Anthropic: Building effective agents, Anthropic: Demystifying evals.

03 / AI FOR SPECIFIC BUSINESS WORKFLOWS

Does the product own a painful job, not just an AI feature?

Customer and why now

Vertical teams are willing to adopt AI when it improves a high-frequency, expensive workflow and fits existing accountability.

Where value could accrue

Distribution, proprietary process context, measurable ROI and a workflow that becomes harder to replace with each use.

Counterargument

Incumbent systems and general model platforms can add the same feature quickly.

Pre-seed / seed evidence

Buyer urgency, repeated use, willingness to pay, implementation effort, human intervention and customer outcomes.

Invalidation: usage is episodic, ROI is unmeasured or the product requires too much bespoke implementation to scale.

Starting sources: Bessemer: Vertical AI principles, Seedcamp investment FAQ, Antler India.
