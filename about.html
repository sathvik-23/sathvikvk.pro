Operator case / Perfstaq

From captured context to a reviewable action.

Building a product sharpens the questions I ask about workflow boundaries, integration cost and failure recovery.

Inspect the public product ↗
Problem
Founder context arrives through conversations and notes, while the resulting work lives across separate business tools.
My action
Built and iterated AI product and workflow systems at Perfstaq across product design, AI tooling and commercial experimentation. The current public product presents captured context, workspace applications and action approvals.
Inspectable output
Perfstaq’s live product presentation describes a Mac capture layer, business workspace and connected assistant actions. Product claims remain attributed to the product; this case does not establish independent performance results.
Evidenced result
A public, inspectable product artifact exists. Revenue, retention and customer outcome metrics are not established by that artifact.
Relevance to diligence
Ask where the human still intervenes, whether approvals protect meaningful actions, what integration costs and how a failed action is recovered.

Research practice

Work close to the problem.

My earlier DeepTech work includes a 48-company research universe, three screens and a CynLr scenario model. That work remains available as a historical exercise in the research archive.

My current questions concern memory and context, reliable AI deployment and business workflows. I turn those themes into company-specific tests and revise the view as evidence changes.
