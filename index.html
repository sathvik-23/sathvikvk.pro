01 / THE AI BOTTLENECK THESIS

When intelligence gets cheap, the constraint moves.

The investable question is not only who builds the best model. It is where the system gets stuck when intelligence becomes abundant.

I map the bottlenecks that convert technical progress into durable economic value—and test whether a company owns the constraint, has a credible path through it, and fits the fund that would have to back it.
