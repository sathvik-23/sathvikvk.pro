THE THESIS

Value migrates toward the next scarce constraint.

When the marginal cost of intelligence collapses, the winning question is no longer “who has the smartest model?” It becomes “who owns the bottleneck that intelligence cannot wish away?”

That lens keeps the work close to founders: what is genuinely difficult to build now, what evidence should exist at this stage, and what must the next round prove?
