Methodology

Make the evidence inspectable.

Dates and funding stage

A round label is the last public financing announcement located in the sources, not a representation of current private capitalization or an open round. Announcement, source access and editorial review dates are separate. Month-only dates remain month-only.

Evidence and judgment

Company and investor claims remain attributed. Public announcements establish what was disclosed, not independent technical performance or verified customer results. The screens contain provisional analytical conclusions and proposed tests; no experiment is implied to have been run.

Coverage and counts

India, UAE, Europe (including the UK) and USA memberships follow cited headquarters or operating-location evidence. Incorporation, nationality and target markets do not establish HQ. Unique company totals differ from overlapping region counts. Conflict holds and stale financing entries are excluded from main pool counts.

Unknowns and conflicts

Missing values remain null in the dataset. Conflicting amount and stage claims are retained rather than silently reconciled. Founder conversations, customers, revenue or diligence access are never inferred from public profiles.

Research access

Relationships default to public research only. This portfolio is independent research alongside founder/operator work.
