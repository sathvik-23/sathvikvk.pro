PRE-SEED · FOUNDER INSIGHT

Can the founder see the constraint before the market does?

Why this founder, why now?

At pre-seed, the central question is whether the founder has unusual insight into a hard problem before the market has made it legible.

What evidence exists?

Technical depth, speed of learning, early customer conversations and a crisp explanation of the constraint are the relevant evidence—not scale metrics that do not exist yet.

What remains assumption?

That the insight can become a repeatable product and that the founder can recruit the missing technical and commercial capability.

Next round must prove

Repeated customer pull, a narrowing wedge and evidence that the founder’s learning loop compounds.

SEED · PERCEPTYNE · WATCH

Are customers demonstrating meaningful demand?

Verified context

C2i is tracked as a semiconductor company in the public-source universe. My assessment is Watch; this is not a claim about its internal maturity.

Evidence today

The relevant evidence is working silicon, a credible design-in path and proof that the power bottleneck is painful enough for OEMs to change behaviour.

Kill question

Can the architecture move from technical demonstration to repeatable OEM adoption without unacceptable qualification or manufacturing risk?

Decision rule

Wait for working silicon plus credible OEM design-in. Proceed only when both are visible in primary evidence.

SERIES A · C2I SEMICONDUCTORS · WATCH

Can adoption, delivery and economics become repeatable?

Verified context

C2i is recorded as a Series A semiconductor company in the public-source universe. That verified funding stage is separate from my assessment of business maturity.

Strongest evidence

A focused semiconductor bottleneck and a public financing context. The customer and product evidence still needs to be tested beyond public materials.

Biggest unknown

Whether working silicon converts into credible OEM design-in, qualification progress and repeatable gross-margin economics.

What changes my mind

Primary evidence of working silicon plus an OEM design-in that can convert to production volume. Until then, Watch.
